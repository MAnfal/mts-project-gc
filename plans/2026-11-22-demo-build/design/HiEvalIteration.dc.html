Iteration 06 · Nov 22 Promoted to live

Let the filter read the most relevant passages, not just the abstract

Why

Error analysis on the tuning weeks showed the filter was the only step losing real rules. It missed the passenger power-bank limit every time, because the limit sits deep in the full text.

Change

For the top 20 candidates, split the full text into ~1,200-character passages, rank them, and give the filter the best 3. Same model, prompt and cut-off.
